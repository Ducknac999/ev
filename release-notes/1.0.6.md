E.V's voice stays steady while it talks, and the notch keeps working when you close or quit E.V.

## Features
- **Close E.V, keep the notch.** ⌘W or the close button hides E.V's window — it leaves the Dock and ⌘Tab, and everything keeps running. ⌘Q closes the app but keeps the notch: clipboard history (⌘⇧C), dictation, music and ☕ keep working. Click E.V in the Dock (or Open E.V in the notch) to bring the window back.
- **Quit completely from the notch.** The new ⏻ button in the notch (click twice) or the E.V icon in the menu bar quits E.V, notch and all.
- **Open E.V at login,** so the notch and clipboard are there as soon as your Mac starts. Settings → General → Closing and quitting, or the setup's Notch step.
- Settings → General → Closing and quitting: choose whether ⌘Q keeps the notch or quits completely.

## Fixes
- **E.V's voice no longer dips while it talks.** With headphones it stays at full volume for the whole answer, whatever the microphone hears (typing, "mm", a cough, the TV). Only "Hey E.V" cuts it off — then it listens for your question.
- **Every sentence at the same loudness,** so the volume never jumps from one sentence to the next.
- On speakers, a voice clearly over E.V turns it down a little with a smooth fade (instead of almost to silence), then back up.
