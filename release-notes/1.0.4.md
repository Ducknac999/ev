E.V gets its own notch: answers, approvals, music with lyrics, a drop box and ☕ — right at the top of your screen.

## Features
- **The E.V notch.** A small black notch (a pill on Macs without one) that shows what E.V heard and answered, asks for approvals with Approve / Skip, and lists files to pick from. Hover it to open Chat, Music and Drop box.
- **Chat panels on or off.** Turn them off and E.V lives in the notch — hover it to type, or use Talk mode. The Dock icon still opens E.V's window.
- **Music card with synced lyrics.** Song, cover, progress and ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ for whatever's playing (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music in a browser…), with lyrics from LRCLIB that follow the song. Lyrics for the last 100 songs are kept (about 0.5 MB), or never — your choice.
- **Drop box.** Drag files onto the notch to keep them handy, then drag them out — one at a time or all at once — into any app or folder. Files stay where they are; nothing is copied.
- **☕ Caffeine.** Keeps your Mac awake until you click it again. Optional: even with the lid closed (asks for your password once), turn off after a time, or below a battery level. If E.V quits or crashes, sleep is turned back on.
- **Find my file, instantly.** "Find my resume", "open my latest PDF", "the essay from last week": Spotlight, ranked — no AI, no tokens. A clear match opens; otherwise pick from the best few ("the second one"). E.V remembers your pick. The AI uses the same one-step search for harder requests.
- The quick-panel shortcut opens the notch's chat when the E.V notch is on. Vorssaint's notch is still an option (Settings → General → Notch).

## Fixes
- Spoken answers that were sometimes silent after music paused: E.V wakes or restarts its audio before speaking and follows a changed output device (headphones, USB, AirPods).
- A greeting before a question ("Hello, what time is it?") no longer makes E.V treat it as a command.
- E.V's voice starts at full volume for every answer.
