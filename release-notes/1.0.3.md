E.V now talks the same way everywhere, knows when music is really playing, and reminders can't be missed.

## Features
- One rule for talking: typed requests are answered in text, spoken questions out loud. "…out loud" / "…quietly" always win.
- Spoken commands ("next song", "pause", "open Spotify") get a chime and a line in the notch instead of talking.
- E.V knows what's really playing from macOS Now Playing (what Control Center shows): audio tools like FineTune or a paused tab no longer count as music. Music is paused for a spoken answer, checked, and resumed after — only what E.V paused.
- Calls (Zoom, FaceTime, Discord…) are never talked over.
- Under each answer, a note says why it was or wasn't spoken.
- 🔇 Mute in the top bar: nothing is said, everything shows in the notch.
- Reminders go into your Mac's Calendar too, so they ring even when E.V is closed.
- Reminders set by voice are read back (day, date, time, how long until); typed ones get a chime and the notch. If the time is unclear, E.V asks first.
- Reminders missed while E.V was closed are announced when it opens.

## UI
- Top bar: Style (Full / Compact) and Show (Always / When the cursor reaches the top) are separate. In full screen the bar slides in under the Mac's menu bar. ⌥⌘B shows or hides it.
- Settings → Voice → Replies explains and lets you change when E.V talks.

## Fixes
- Per-chat speaker buttons and hold-to-talk's own speak switch are replaced by the one rule (they caused E.V to talk at random).
- E.V no longer presses play/pause on a guess, so it can't start your music by accident.
- Reminders, approvals and failed tasks are always announced, even with sound off in a chat.
