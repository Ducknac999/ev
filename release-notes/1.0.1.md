A new look, and two fixes from the first round of testing.

## UI
- New Midnight design: deep navy with a soft light beam, glass cards with rounded corners, one blue accent and pill buttons.
- New home screen: start an E.V Chat, Claude Code, Codex or a Terminal from big cards; recently closed sessions underneath.
- Normal, readable text everywhere (no more all-caps monospace labels).
- Updates now have their own tab in Settings.

## Fixes
- The microphone permission now turns green as soon as you allow it.
- "Gemini chat" is now "E.V Chat", whatever AI you use.
- The update check says which version is newest and when it last checked.
