Clipboard history in the notch, instant actions with no AI, Handy-style dictation into any app, and voice that's cleaned on your Mac before E.V sees it.

## Features
- **Clipboard history in the notch.** Everything you copy — text, links, images, files — in the notch's new Clipboard tab. Press ⌘⇧C in any app, type to search, Enter pastes it where you were (⌥Enter only copies, ⌘1–⌘9 for the first nine), pin the ones you reuse. Kept on your Mac only; passwords from password managers are never kept, and you can list apps to skip. Change the shortcut in Settings → Notch.
- **A cleaner notch.** Icons beside the camera instead of text tabs (Chat, Music, Drop box, Clipboard, Dictation on the left; Talk mode, ☕, chat panels and Open E.V on the right), and clipboard items and dictations as cards with the app they came from.
- **The notch steps aside in full-screen apps,** so it never covers an app's top bar. Push your pointer to the top edge to slide it out; approvals, answers and ⌘⇧C still show it. Settings → Notch.
- **Settings, reorganized and searchable.** Clear sections with icons (AI, Voice & keyboard, Notch, Safety & Mac, Activity, App), every shortcut on one Shortcuts page, switches instead of checkboxes, and a search (⌘F) that finds any setting from part of a word, a typo or another word for it ("keep awake" → Caffeine).
- **Setup asks about the notch.** A new step shows what the notch looks like and lets you turn it, or any of its sections, on or off.
- **Instant actions, however you say them.** Volume, play/pause/skip, dark mode, opening an app, finding a file, math and the time are done right away with no AI and no tokens — "crank it all the way up", "skip this", "what's 15% of 80". Your Mac's on-device model decides (macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence; other Macs keep the regular instant commands). Requests your AI handled with one simple action become instant next time. Settings → Instant actions.
- **Spoken math** without AI: "two times three times four", "half of 250", "12 squared", "square root of 144".
- **Dictation into any app.** Hold Right Option (or the key you choose), talk, let go: the words are pasted where your cursor is, and your clipboard comes back right after. Transcribed on your Mac (Parakeet) while you talk, so it's ready the moment you let go. Say "comma", "period", "new line". A space after each dictation keeps them apart.
- **Dictation history.** Your last 20 dictations: the notch's new Dictation tab and the E.V icon in the menu bar ("Copy last dictation"). If there was nowhere to type, the notch offers to copy it — nothing is lost.
- **Your voice, cleaned first.** Everything you say is tidied on the Mac before E.V sees it — no "um", stutters or misheard names, "Friday, actually Thursday" → "Thursday" — so requests are shorter and cost fewer tokens.
- **Your dictionary.** Names and words E.V should always spell right (it catches sound-alikes). Settings → Dictation, or say "add … to my dictionary".
- **Hands-free.** Double-tap ⌘ to turn Talk mode on or off from anywhere. Say "type …", "send it", "new line", "delete that", "click Submit", "take a screenshot" (or "… into Slack").
- **Polished dictation (optional).** Apple's on-device model also fixes self-corrections ("to Sam, no, to Maya" → "to Maya").

## Fixes
- Talk mode works the first time from any app, even with E.V's window hidden: the microphone and E.V's voice now run natively.
- E.V's voice plays sentence after sentence with no gaps and follows your current output (headphones, AirPods, USB).
- A model writing its tool call as text (the "parameter name…" answers) is never shown or spoken; E.V retries it.
- "max volume", "100% volume" and "a hundred percent volume" are instant.
- Simple actions confirm what happened ("Set volume 100.") instead of the action's name.
- Volume and dark mode are always ready to the AI, so they no longer need an extra step (and tokens).
