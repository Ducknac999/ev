E.V tells you exactly what it did, asks its questions in the notch, and finishes every job with a clear report. It can now work inside web pages in the browser you already use.

## Features
- **Questions in the notch.** When E.V needs an answer, the question appears in the notch with its choices, Skip, or a box for your own answer. ⌃⌥A picks the first choice and ⌃⌥D skips. Answer anywhere and it disappears everywhere.
- **"Waiting for you."** While a question or approval is waiting, the running step and the progress strip say so (in amber), and the notch chat shows what it's waiting on instead of "…".
- **Settings → Browsers.** Pick the browser you use and turn on fast control (or add the E.V extension) so E.V can read and work in your open web pages. The setup has a new Browser step.
- **Works inside web pages.** E.V looks at a page and acts on it one step at a time, with a result for every action. It answers page popups (alert, confirm, prompt; confirm stays on Cancel unless you approve) and picks from custom dropdown lists.
- **Frames from videos.** E.V can grab up to 10 frames from a video at the times you name (or spread evenly), asking first before sending them to a vision model. Captions now carry time marks.

## Fixes
- **Honest answers.** The fact-check can no longer turn a true "done" into "I can't do that". E.V knows which browsers it actually controls and won't send you to do something it can do itself.
- **A final report every time.** After any job, including after Stop, an error or the step limit, you get what was done, what didn't work and what couldn't be confirmed. An action with no visible change is reported as "not confirmed".
- **No more empty promises.** A reply that only says "I'll…" without doing anything is sent back to actually do it.
- **No guessing loops.** A slow look is retried once; an action is never re-sent blind after a timeout; repeating the same action that changes nothing is stopped.
- **No made-up values.** E.V uses what you said or what it read, or it asks.
- Event, reminder, note and draft changes made through connections are read back before E.V says they're done.
